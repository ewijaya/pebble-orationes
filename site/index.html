Open in Pebble
  1. Tap the button above. Choose Pebble if prompted.
  2. Check the listing's version, then tap Add To Watch if offered.
  3. Return to My Apps → Orationes to check the artwork and Settings.

Keep your working installation. You do not need to uninstall Orationes to try this link.

If Pebble does not open

Open this webpage in Chrome on your Android phone, then tap the button again. Pebble must already be installed.

You can also try the direct Pebble link from this page.

If you see an error or no Add To Watch button, keep your installation and share a screenshot of that screen.
