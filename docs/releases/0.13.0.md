# Orationes v0.13.0

- New: Meal Prayers, under All Prayers → Daily Prayer and available as a
  shortcut. It contains the Blessing Before Meals, with its midday and evening
  additions, followed by Grace After Meals.
- Jump to section moves between the Blessing, each addition, and Grace.
- Versicles and responses use V./R., matching the other prayers.

Existing prayer wording, shortcuts, saved-settings formats, and store artwork
are unchanged.
