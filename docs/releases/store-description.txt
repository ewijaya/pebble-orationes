Make time for prayer. On your wrist. Available offline.

Orationes is a Catholic prayer book for Pebble Time 2. Large text and clear contrast help you focus on the words. Your prayers stay on your watch, ready without a phone or internet connection.

PRAY THROUGHOUT YOUR DAY
Start with Latin Preces, Holy Rosary, Regina Caeli, Angelus, and Memorare. Explore Come, Holy Spirit, the Litany of Humility, 92 Aspirations, 14 Prayer Cards, the Litany of Loreto, psalms, prayers before work, preparation for Confession, and more. The Rosary mystery reference previews today's set and weekday before opening it. All Mysteries separates each set's name from its weekday details.

KEEP YOUR PRAYERS CLOSE
Choose and arrange up to seven shortcuts to individual prayers or collections. Personalize them on the watch or through Settings in the Pebble mobile app. Browse All Prayers and press Select to open a prayer directly; hold Select and release for Open/Pin options. A readable first-use hint explains the gesture, and Settings → Menu Help keeps it available.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
With Remember Place On, opening a prayer from any menu resumes its saved position, or starts at the beginning if none exists. With it Off, prayers always start at the beginning and saved places are cleared. Continue displays your last saved prayer name prominently and opens it directly. Recent Prayers keeps separate places for up to eight prayers. Enable Continue First at the top of watch Settings or in phone Settings to put Continue above your shortcuts; the setting stays synchronized between both. Both Settings pages show their bundled app version at the bottom.

READ YOUR WAY
New installations start with Dark appearance, Ocean title accent, and Lime navigation highlight. Choose Large or Extra Large text, Light or Dark appearance, and independent title and navigation colors. Existing preferences are preserved on upgrade. Prayer text retains strong black-and-white contrast, with clearer response blocks and stanza spacing. The Litany of Humility attribution stays in small print. Compact headers, submenu chevrons, and a narrow list-position indicator make menus easier to browse. The watch-local Compact Menus option fits short rows into 48 pixels instead of the spacious 54-pixel default, keeping full-size text and expanding longer names. The Preces blessing cross is supported in both text sizes.

RESTORE YOUR DEFAULTS
One Restore Defaults action resets shortcuts and shared preferences: Dark/Ocean/Lime colors, Large text, Remember Place On, Continue First Off, and the noon reminder Off with a 10-second duration. Saved prayer positions are kept. On the phone, tap Save Settings to apply the reset. Watch Restore Defaults also turns Compact Menus off; phone changes preserve that watch-local choice.

MOVE AT YOUR PACE
Swipe to scroll, tap Up or Down, or hold either button to move faster. Press Select while reading for Start again or Jump to section where available. Aspirations offers ten jump groups covering all 92 entries. Double-press Select while reading to return to your watchface.

PAUSE AT NOON
Enable an optional vibration reminder for Angelus or Regina Caeli, selected automatically for the season.
