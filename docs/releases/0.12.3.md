# Orationes v0.12.3

- Reduce Bluetooth traffic: launching Orationes no longer asks the watch to send
  its full settings to the phone. Phone Settings still requests current watch
  values when opened; if the watch does not reply within two seconds, cached
  values are clearly labelled.
- Settings saved on the phone but not yet confirmed by the watch are still resent
  when Orationes launches.

Watch screens, controls, prayer wording, saved-settings formats, and store
artwork are unchanged.
